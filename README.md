# ⚖️ AI-Powered Legal Aid Chatbot

> An AI-powered chatbot designed to improve access to basic legal information in Kenya.

## 📌 Project Overview

The AI-Powered Legal Aid Chatbot is a proposed software solution designed to help users access basic legal information through a conversational interface.

The project focuses on making legal information more accessible to individuals who may face challenges navigating traditional legal support channels.

The system is being designed with **Kituo Cha Sheria** as the target organisation and focuses on selected areas of Kenyan law.

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Access to legal information can be difficult for people who may not know where to find reliable information or how to navigate legal support services.

This project explores how Artificial Intelligence can provide accessible, structured preliminary legal information while directing complex matters to qualified legal professionals.

## ✨ Key Features

- 💬 Conversational legal information assistant
- 🇰🇪 Focus on the Kenyan legal context
- 🌍 English interaction
- 🏠 Tenant rights information
- 💼 Employment dispute information
- 🌱 Land ownership information
- 👨‍⚖️ Escalation of complex matters to qualified legal professionals
- 🗄️ Structured legal information database

## 🧠 AI Component

The AI component is being designed to interpret user questions and provide relevant responses based on the project's legal knowledge base.

The system is intended to provide **general legal information rather than replace professional legal advice**.
